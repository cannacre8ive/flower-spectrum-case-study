A concrete engineering decision

The source outlives
the model.

The original classifier’s Mt. Hood Magic regression preserved all 19 positive source rows, including 0.03% trans-Phytol outside its mapping. Total source mass reconciled to 3.41%; modeled mass remained 3.38%.

This is a meaningful example of creative problem solving: extraction and interpretation were separated so an incomplete model could not silently rewrite the evidence. The case study preserves that principle.

The success rubric.

25%

Source integrity

Rows, units and totals remain inspectable. Missing evidence blocks approval.

25%

System consistency

The approved record drives every specimen without rekeying or silent changes.

20%

Clear explanation

A newcomer can distinguish measured chemistry from a model interpretation.

20%

Visual craft

The identity remains legible across screen, social proportions and print.

10%

Honest claims

Authorship, examples, deployment status and future work are explicit.

An acceptance rubric for the portfolio—not a scored user study or a claim of measured business performance.
